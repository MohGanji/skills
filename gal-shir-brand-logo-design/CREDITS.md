# Credits & attribution

## The designer

This skill is modeled on the design principles, method, and public work of
**[Gal Shir](https://galshir.com)** (@galshirart) — brand & logo designer, founding-team
brand designer at Lemonade, author of the "60 Tips for Logo Design" e-book, founder of
Color Hunt. The taste, vocabulary, and method encoded here are his; the `logokit` SDK is
our translation of that craft into a single reproducible vector medium.

Gal Shir is **not affiliated with this skill and has not endorsed it.**

## The example marks (`references/inspirations/`)

Each inspiration folder studies a real brand mark **designed by Gal Shir**. For each we
include a downloaded copy of the publicly-published logo (`original.svg`/`original.png`)
and **Gal Shir's own project showcase images** from his portfolio (`images/`, sourced
from `galshir.com/#<brand>`), plus an independent analysis. Everything is a real,
publicly-sourced reference — there are no reconstructions or generated assets in
`references/`.

## Method note

The per-brand analyses were produced by a **blind, multi-agent reverse-engineering
method**: three independent investigators (brand truth, designer commentary, and a pure
visual read of the SVG with no brand context) working without sight of each other, then a
consolidation testing whether their conclusions align. Alignment scores and honest
divergences are reported per mark — they reflect our reconstruction of Gal's likely
thinking, not statements by him.
