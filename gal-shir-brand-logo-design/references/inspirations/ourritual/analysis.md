# OurRitual — reverse-engineered (blind 3-agent method)

*Produced by **three independent, blind investigations** that never saw each other's work
— a brand-truth reader (the brand's own site), a Gal-Shir-commentary reader, and a pure
visual reader who deconstructed the actual logo **with no brand context** — then a
consolidation testing whether they align. Method note:
[../../gal-shir-principles.md](../../gal-shir-principles.md).*

- **Alignment (blind read vs. brand truth vs. Gal's intent):** **strong**
- **Idea move:** Overlap-as-thesis: two initials built from twin primitives, slid into a Venn intersection whose shared center is drawn as its own element -- so the monogram literally enacts "two become one." (A fusion of "monogram-as-metaphor" and "the meaning lives in the overlap.")

## 1. Brand truth (blind)

OurRitual is a relationship-care platform positioned as "a new approach to couples therapy, combining expert guidance with a tailored digital experience." Its founding conviction: "the most treasured part of our lives deserve the same ongoing care and investment we give everything else that truly matters." Founded 2021 (originally "Hey Ritual") by CEO David Pruwer with Nir Shtern and Gilad Meir, out of a personal experience of divorce and "relationship loneliness."

The central reframe is the key to the whole brand: **"Love and connection are not just feelings you have. They are practices, rituals, you nurture."** They explicitly reject quick fixes -- "There are plenty of short-term fixes for the bad weeks. OurRitual is the place for continuous care for the long arc." Mission: "help the world connect, one relationship at a time." Vision: "What if relationships were just better?"

- **Audience:** people improving, repairing, or strengthening romantic relationships -- reachable "solo or together." Deliberately inclusive ("We're Here for You, No Matter Who You Are"), with named programs for couples, solo individuals, long-distance, LGBTQ+, parents, and military couples. Modern, digital-first, busy; notably reaches men who feel "relationship loneliness."
- **Identity:** modern, tech-enabled relationship care -- "Built by Relationship Experts. Designed for Real Life." "Technology that's guided by humans," pairing an AI guide ("Ora") with 300+ licensed experts and marquee clinical advisors (Dr. Orna Guralnik).
- **Values:** ongoing care over quick fixes; accessibility; inclusivity; flexibility; human-guided technology; intentional, practiced love; practicality and sustainable results; a larger social purpose ("strengthen the foundations of society").
- **Personality:** warm, encouraging, empowering, yet grounded and credible. Intimate first-person-plural voice ("We're here for you"), validating the courage to seek help, optimistic and non-clinical -- softening therapy's stigma while keeping authority. "Connect. Communicate. Grow."

## 2. What Gal said

Effectively nothing about the mark's meaning. **Confidence: none.** His only accessible public statement (LinkedIn, July 2024):

> "Proud to have designed the new visual identity of OurRitual! It was an honor working with David and Nir and creating an identity for such a unique, warm, and fun brand. See more on https://galshir.com"

He describes the *brand's* character -- "unique, warm, and fun" -- and credits the founders, but explains no concept, no symbolism, no letterform rationale. An Instagram reel titled "Logo design for OurRitual" exists but its caption is behind a login wall and could not be retrieved. Nothing on galshir.com, Dribbble, X, or the PRINT interview. So his stated intent gives us exactly one usable data point: the intended emotional register is **warm and fun**, not clinical.

## 3. The mark, read blind

An analyst who never knew the brand saw (confidence: **high**):

> "Two overlapping coral-red rounded shapes forming a compact monogram." A soft vertical capsule on the left (reads as **O**, or a petal/leaf); a bowl-plus-diagonal-leg form on the right (reads as **R**, or a stepping figure). Where they cross, "a darker maroon almond/lens sits -- a Venn-diagram overlap that also reads as a leaf, a petal, or a seed." Overall: "two bodies leaning into each other, or two petals sharing a core."

- **Construction:** three filled paths, no strokes. Two near-identical rounded primitives (~equal radii, shared top anchor) offset horizontally -- modular, rhythmic. The maroon vesica is drawn as **its own path**, i.e. the overlap is a deliberate, named element, not an accident.
- **Geometry:** chunky, confident lockup (~64% cap-height). The left form is bilaterally symmetric; the right form breaks symmetry with a diagonal leg planting a sharp point at the baseline -- "dynamic asymmetry / forward lean." Deliberate soft-vs-sharp tension: everything round except one angular terminal.
- **Colour:** two warm values -- saturated coral/salmon (#FF5B4C), "warm, energetic, friendly, slightly playful rather than aggressive"; the overlap a deep maroon/oxblood (#8C2C38, the coral darkened -> depth). Wordmark a soft plum near-black (#1E131F). "No blues, no cool tones -- entirely warm, human, intimate."
- **Implied concept:** "Union / togetherness... two becoming one, partnership, relationship, or connection, with the darker overlap marking the meaningful thing born from that union." Leaf/petal/seed layers on "organic growth and nurturing"; the forward-leaning leg adds "movement and progress -- a relationship going somewhere."
- **Implied values:** "warm, human, intimate, optimistic... care, empathy, approachability and emotional safety rather than corporate hardness"; "collaboration, mutual support and connection"; "gentle but purposeful." Personality: "friendly, supportive, contemporary, quietly confident, relationship-centered."

## 4. Consolidation -- do they align?

**Alignment: strong.**

The decisive result: a reader with **zero** knowledge of the brand recovered its core thesis unprompted. [C]'s "two becoming one, partnership, relationship, connection" is the visual form of [A]'s entire reason to exist -- "help the world connect, one relationship at a time," love as something two people "nurture." That is a clean, high-confidence hit.

Agreements, layered:

- **Core meaning (strong):** [C] "union / togetherness / relationship" == [A]'s mission. The mark *performs* the brand's one big idea.
- **Emotional tone (three-way):** [C] read "warm, human, intimate, safe, non-corporate" from the coral and the roundness; [A] is "warm, encouraging, non-clinical, it-takes-bravery"; and [B] -- Gal's own words -- is "warm, and fun." All three point the same way.
- **Secondary layer (partial, more speculative):** [C]'s leaf/petal/seed = "nurture and growth" maps to [A]'s "practices you nurture," "Grow," and its education framing; [C]'s forward-leaning leg = "progress" maps to the brand's growth arc.

Divergences -- honestly, one contradiction-free gap and one genuine uncertainty:

- **Scope gap (expected, not a failure):** [C] does **not** recover [A]'s heavily-stressed strategic pillars -- "continuous care for the long arc / not a quick fix," the literal *repeated-practice* sense of the word **"ritual"** (cadence, repetition), the human+AI hybrid model, clinical credibility, and segment inclusivity. A compact two-shape monogram cannot and should not carry operational strategy; that work belongs to copy and the wider system. Worth naming plainly: the one word in the company's name -- *ritual*, meaning something you do again and again -- is the part the mark does **not** encode. It says "connection," not "ongoing practice."
- **Genuine uncertainty:** the **O/R letterform** reading. [C] offered it as one reading among several. The brand *name* corroborates it, but neither [A] nor [B] confirms Gal intended initials. Treat "it's a monogram" as likely-but-unconfirmed.
- **The Gal gap:** [B] states no concept at all (confidence: none). So we cannot say the union symbolism *matches his stated intent* -- only that his one adjective ("warm, fun") is consistent with the read. The strong alignment is between the **blind read** and the **brand truth**; Gal's intent is inferred, not evidenced.

Net: where the mark makes a claim, the claim is true to the brand and legible without a caption. It carries the emotional and relational **core** with high fidelity and deliberately leaves the strategic **detail** to the rest of the identity.

## 5. Reverse-engineered process & lesson

**Likely path from brief to mark:**

1. **Distill to one word.** From a maximalist brief (therapy alternative, AI+human hybrid, inclusive, flexible, ongoing care) Gal reduced to the irreducible core: *two people, connected.* He refused to illustrate the product (no app, no chat bubble, no clinician) and made the relationship itself the subject.
2. **Mine the name.** "OurRitual" hands over O and R. He treated them not as type but as two objects that can physically relate in space.
3. **Find the overlap gesture (the whole idea).** Build the O and R from two near-identical rounded primitives and slide them into a Venn intersection, so the composition literally *performs* togetherness. Draw the intersection as its own darker path -- promoting the shared center to a named element (the bond, the "us"), not an accident of layering.
4. **Warm it up.** Coral/salmon over pure red (energy without aggression); every corner rounded (care, safety); a warm humanist serif in soft plum-black -- de-stigmatizing therapy, matching the "warm, fun" character he named.
5. **Add one point of intention.** Plant a single sharp diagonal leg to the baseline so the mark leans and steps forward -- momentum ("Grow"), and an antidote to cuteness.
6. **Test the reads.** Confirm it survives as a monogram (O+R), as two people leaning in, and as a petal/seed -- multiple valid readings that all reinforce one theme, none fighting it. Ship.

**Idea move:** *Overlap-as-thesis* -- twin initials slid into a Venn intersection whose shared center is drawn as its own element, so the monogram enacts "two become one."

**Lesson for designing in his style:** Encode the ONE irreducible human relationship at the heart of the brand, then let geometry *perform* it rather than a symbol you must decode. Concretely: (1) reduce the brief to a single word before drawing; pick the emotional core over the feature list. (2) Mine the name -- initials are free objects that can relate spatially. (3) Put the meaning in a *relationship between shapes* (overlap, lean, share). (4) Carry tone in palette and curvature -- warm coral, rounded corners -- to signal safety. (5) Add exactly one point of tension amid the softness to inject intention. (6) Accept that a mark carries emotional truth, not strategy; don't cram the whole brief in -- let copy and the system do the rest. And aim, as here, for a mark that reads three ways that all mean the same thing.
