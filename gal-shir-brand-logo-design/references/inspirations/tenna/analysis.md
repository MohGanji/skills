# Tenna — reverse-engineered (blind 3-agent method)

*Produced by **three independent, blind investigations** that never saw each other's work
— a brand-truth reader (the brand's own site), a Gal-Shir-commentary reader, and a pure
visual reader who deconstructed the actual logo **with no brand context** — then a
consolidation testing whether they align. Method note:
[../../gal-shir-principles.md](../../gal-shir-principles.md).*

- **Alignment (blind read vs. brand truth vs. Gal's intent):** **partial**
- **Idea move:** Kinetic wordmark with a dual-read accent — "the name as the moving object." Make the type itself the thing being launched/tracked (aggressive italic on a fixed engineered angle), then trail it with a repeated accent device whose spacing is math-tuned to encode acceleration. The device is a double-entendre: it reads as speed lines to anyone, and as a signal/spectrum trace to those who know the domain. Meaning is carried by motion and rhythm, not by a pictorial icon. Supporting moves: single loud spot color as the entire brand signature, and total geometric discipline (one shared shear angle, zero curves) to convert "dynamism" into "engineered precision."

## 1. Brand truth (blind)

Tenna Systems (founded 2023, formerly "Tip & Cue Inc.") is a defense-tech startup building a **software-driven spectrum resilience / spectrum-intelligence platform** — hardware-free and hardware-agnostic. It fuses airborne, space, and terrestrial RF data into "a clear, real-time picture of the electromagnetic battlespace." Founded by a team with ~15 years in modern warfare, "forged in actual conflict zones," HQ across Tel Aviv and New York, $13.5M seed.

- **Audience:** warfighters, EW/SIGINT operators, defense acquisition buyers, and operators of drones/satellites/aircraft/autonomous systems. Named targets include the U.S. DoD, Army, Air Force, allied nations, and the Israeli MoD/Air Force.
- **Identity:** the modern, software-native, AI/data-fusion **challenger to bulky legacy EW hardware**. Self-labels: "the Signal Source of Truth," "the AccuWeather of Electronic Warfare." Products: Arena (spectrum visibility), Tracer (precision geolocation), Halo (embedded EW resilience). Tagline: **"All Systems Go."**
- **Values:** real-time truth and clarity; mission-criticality ("failure isn't an option"); battle-tested credibility ("battle-hardened," "proven in high-profile operations"); speed and flexibility over inflexible hardware ("months, not years"); protection and operational control.
- **Strategy:** the software-native, hardware-free alternative — "Stop waiting for new hardware. Start using the data you already have." Defense-first go-to-market.
- **Personality:** authoritative, mission-critical, battle-tested; peer-to-peer voice of operators who have "been there."

> Identity note: this is **Tenna Systems (tennasys.com)**, the defense-tech startup — NOT tenna.com, the unrelated construction fleet-management company.

## 3. The mark, read blind

A tight, **aggressively italic wordmark** of five heavy uppercase white letterforms, preceded on the left by a cluster of **five parallel diagonal stripes in hot magenta (FF0055)**. One long, low horizontal band (~7.35:1). No curves anywhere — all straight segments, hard corners, sharp triangular counters.

- **Geometry:** strict italic on a clean **30-degree** shear; every terminal flat-cut to that same angle. The five accent stripes are near-identical in width but their **gaps accelerate** (4.71 → 6.29 → 7.85 → 9.42), an arithmetic ramp reading as acceleration / perspective / motion blur.
- **Color:** two colors only, high contrast. White does the reading; hot pink is the entire brand signature. Built for a **dark background** (canvas fill none).
- **Negative space:** knocked-out triangular counters (sharpest at the A); the widening slivers between stripes imply a fading motion trail moving left-to-right into the name. No pictorial icon hidden — the concealed reading is **purely kinetic**.
- **Implied concept:** forward motion and speed — a name being launched/driven rightward with a pink slipstream. Propulsion, tracking, performance.
- **Implied values:** bold, energetic, modern, precise, disruptive, "youthful… rather than corporate conservative."

**Confidence: high.**

## 4. Consolidation — do they align?

**Verdict: PARTIAL alignment.** The mark recovers the *feeling and strategy* of the brand strongly, but not its *literal subject or sector*. And because Gal's intent [B] is unverifiable, "did he do his job" can only be tested indirectly, mark-vs-brand.

**Strong agreement ([A] ↔ [C]):**
- **Disruptive-challenger personality.** [C]: "assertive, fast-moving, disruptive… rather than corporate conservative." [A]: "modern, software-native challenger to bulky legacy hardware." Near-exact.
- **Real-time / motion.** [C]'s dominant read is velocity, tracking, propulsion. [A] is literally real-time tracking, geolocation (Tracer), and "moving systems always stay connected."
- **Precision / engineering.** [C]'s single-angle flat terminals and sharp counters map onto [A]'s "Precise Emitter Geolocation" and "Signal Source of Truth."
- **Modern over legacy.** The loud hot-pink-on-dark treatment reads as software-native insurgent — exactly [A]'s wedge against "expensive, inflexible" incumbents.

**Divergence (honest):**
- **Literal subject not recovered.** [C] concludes "vehicle / sport / performance" and reads the stripes as **speed lines**. [A] is **spectrum / electronic-warfare defense tech**. A blind viewer would not reach "RF, jamming, SIGINT, battlespace." Notably, those same five parallel slanted lines are a *latent* perfect fit for an **RF signal / spectrum trace** — a second meaning the blind read missed. The mark is recoverable-*adjacent*, not recovered.
- **Sector tone.** Hot pink signals consumer-tech disruption, not defense. This hides the actual sector from a blind reader — a deliberate, strategically defensible "un-defense" choice, but a divergence nonetheless.
- **Gravity absent.** Nothing in [C] recovers [A]'s stakes ("lives are lost," "failure isn't an option"). The mark is confident and fast; it is not somber or martial.

**Net:** the mark is an excellent carrier of Tenna's *strategic feeling* (fast, precise, disruptive, real-time) and a poor carrier of its *literal category* (defense EW). Whether that trade is right depends on brand context supplying the sector the mark intentionally omits.

## 5. Reverse-engineered process & lesson

> Caveat: with [B] empty, this is a plausible reconstruction from the mark against the brief, not a documented process.

**Likely path:**
1. **Distill the dense brief to one verb** — not "spectrum resilience" but *move, precisely, in real time*. Everything serves that.
2. **Reject defense clichés** (shields, crosshairs, radar, camo, dark palette). Tenna's positioning IS "modern challenger vs. legacy hardware," so the logo must not look like legacy defense — licensing a loud, un-military treatment.
3. **Make the wordmark the moving object** — short 5-letter name set in heavy uppercase, italic on a strict engineered 30-degree grid so the slant reads as intention, not sloppiness.
4. **Add one kinetic, dual-read device** — five stripes that are simultaneously **speed lines** and a **signal/spectrum trace**. Tune them: equal width for order, accelerating gaps for velocity.
5. **Constrain hard** — two colors, one loud spot color carrying all the energy, zero curves, every terminal sheared to the same angle so the whole thing feels machined.
6. **Set on dark** for a high-tech product context.

**Idea move:** *Kinetic wordmark with a dual-read accent — "the name as the moving object."* Meaning carried by motion and rhythm, not by a pictorial icon; the accent double-reads as speed (everyone) and signal (insiders).

**Lesson for designing in his style:**
1. **Don't draw an abstract subject — encode its verb.** For un-illustratable briefs (spectrum, resilience), distill to one feeling and build the whole mark around it.
2. **Choose a device that double-reads** — universal feeling plus domain meaning — for depth without losing immediate legibility.
3. **Tune repetition with math, not vibes** — equal width + accelerating gaps is what makes a trail feel alive; deliberate ramps read as intention.
4. **One grid, one accent** — a single shared angle turns "dynamic" into "engineered"; let one loud spot color on a neutral be the entire signature.
5. **Know the trade you're making** — this approach can hide the sector (a blind viewer read "sport," not "defense"). Often the right, deliberate call — but ensure surrounding brand context restores the category the mark omits.
